# Magic modifier consumers

`magic-effect-hooks.js` contains pure rule decisions, not a replacement for Foundry command execution. All its tests run without a Foundry server. **No spell becomes implemented merely because a modifier was saved.** `supportedMagicRules(installedSites)` returns only rules whose listed consumers have actually been installed. With no installed consumers it returns an empty list. `MAGIC_RULE_HOOKS` lists the required sites; `MAGIC_HOOK_SITES` explains them. Unknown rules, including social/world adjudication and ongoing saves without a lifecycle, remain unsupported.

## Storage and lifetime

Store the full audited modifier operation at `actor.system.effects[].magic.operation`. `magic.operations` also accepts several already-active modifier operations. The reader deliberately does not traverse nested future attacks, saving throws, or on-hit effects. The executor must resolve their trigger first. `disabled`, `magic.suppressed`, and `magic.expired` records are ignored.

`effect.modifiers` may contain `unconditionalMagicModifiers(operation)` for backward-compatible display. A predicate, minimum original skill rank, or daily-use limit makes that map empty. Once integrated, `magicModifierSummary(state, context).modifiers` **replaces the existing loop over effect.modifiers**. It reads the canonical operation once, retains ordinary potion/mutagen records, multiplies multiplier fields, and reports missing context instead of applying an unqualified bonus.

Effects still use existing authoritative expiry and source-owned condition removal. Hook outputs are previews until their owning command commits. `magicEffectCommit` returns new effects and removed IDs; callers must run the normal condition-ownership reconciliation for removals and include all writes in the existing compensating transaction. Do not replace conditions with a list derived from effect names.

## Context that cannot be inferred

- Cloak's `detected` is per opponent. `visibilityCauses` may include `yrden`/`moondust`; `invisibilityNegated` is a separate reveal. A global actor flag must not grant every opponent knowledge because one spotted the caster.
- `predicates` such as `animalHandling`, `interrogateSelectedTarget`, `plantOnlyElixirCraftingOrRecovery`, and `chosenAlly` must be resolved from action target/materials/Region selection. No browser payload may assert eligibility without validation.
- Divine Inspiration requires a learned skill and an explicitly selected use. The summary supports `dayKey`, `useLimitedBonus`, and persisted `magic.dailyUse`; it does not itself expend the allowance. Its whole workflow remains unsupported until the daily receipt is committed with the roll.
- Healing sources and `fireId` must identify the actual effect. Harmless Fire never becomes general fire immunity.
- Glide needs `activeAtLanding:true`; effect presence before an interrupted fall is insufficient.
- Silverlight's next-sunrise expiry requires world calendar/GM time; no arbitrary fixed24-hour timer is substituted.

## Audited source decisions

Core pp.103–113 supplies environmental wards, gliding, field restrictions, and White Flame. Tome pp.82–90 supplies conditional checks, Light Feet, Slip Stream, Coating of Fire, Cloak, Empower, Bastion, and Recovery prohibition. Tome pp.93–100 supplies Brand of Withering, Champion, Silverlight, Blood of the Mountain, Totem, Taiga, and Well of Knowledge. These tests use the actual catalog effect plans, not duplicate spell objects, except isolated environmental/conditional cases.

- Repeated ordinary damage resistance never stacks (Core p.154).
- Blood of the Mountain explicitly affects melee parry and doubles ablation on weapons, shields, **and armor** (Tome p.98).
- Silverlight retains wound/death state while waiving its penalties, and has one prevention use for the first reduction below1HP (Tome p.95).
- Cloak lasts until an attack hits; an Awareness success retains+3 attack/defense, while Yrden/Moondust also changes Stealth to+5 (Tome p.85).
- Empower's damage choice adds2d6 only to an already damaging spell; its chance choice changes existing chances; a natural1 causes severity10 (Tome p.85).
- Focusing with a Druidic Totem is a minimum Focus4 within its area, not a second additive focus (Tome p.98).
- No blanket immunity is inferred from descriptive power. Aurora's Breath addresses breathing water; it cannot prevent strangulation.

## Explicitly incomplete rules

Rules absent from `MAGIC_RULE_HOOKS` are not fully handled here. Examples: Silenced's recurring opposed save; Spectral Tether movement/saves; Crystal Stasis phases; Omens' interrupting another d10; Divine Luck's shared pool; eternal fire spreading/submersion; disease onset/cure/rest; recoverable magical death; controlled active attacks; projectile deflection; social commands/memory; summons and world objects. Some helpers return useful partial decisions, such as Hold Tongue's casting restriction, but those decisions do not promote the whole rule.

Invocation learning audit: Core p.123 and Tome p.36 forbid mages' invocations and distinguish their magic from priests'. Tome pp.49/67 and91/96 split priest/druid professions and their lists, and label arch invocations by deity/circle. No explicit cross-list or patron-exclusive learning prohibition was found in those rules; retain the association as source guidance rather than inventing a hard lock. Tome p.74 **does** explicitly forbid gifted laypeople from learning spells/invocations/other magic or using Focus. Gift actors require their separate14-gift procedure, not access to ordinary spell items.
